# Listical Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 1 August 2026**

This policy explains how Studio PDW Ltd ("Listical", "we", "us") collects and uses your personal data when you use the Listical web application at tacular.app and the Listical iOS app (together, "the Service").

We are the data controller for your personal data. We are a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16687130) with our registered office at Flat 73 Boyd Building, 3 Hudson Way, London, England, E16 2GW. You can contact us about anything in this policy at hello@tacular.app.

We comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. If you use the Service from the European Union, the EU GDPR applies to you in the same way.

## What we collect

We collect only what the Service needs to work.

**Account data.** Your email address and a password (stored by our authentication provider in hashed form, never in plain text). Your date of birth, which we collect once at signup solely to confirm that you are 18 or older. We do not use your date of birth for any other purpose.

**Planning data.** The content you create in Listical: your goals, plans, projects, tasks, notes, schedules and related settings such as your chosen colour theme. This content belongs to you and we only store and display it back to you.

**Service records.** If you ask us to delete your account, we keep a minimal audit record of that request so we can demonstrate that we honoured it.

**Technical data.** Our hosting providers process your IP address and standard request information as a necessary part of serving the application and keeping it secure. We do not use analytics services, advertising trackers or third party cookies. The only cookies and local tokens we use are those strictly necessary to keep you signed in.

## Why we process your data and our lawful basis

We process your account data and planning data to provide the Service to you. The lawful basis is performance of a contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)): we cannot provide a planning tool without storing your plans.

We process your date of birth to enforce our minimum age of 18. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in restricting the Service to adults (Article 6(1)(f)), which also serves our compliance with age related data protection rules.

We process deletion audit records and security related technical data on the basis of our legitimate interests in security and in demonstrating compliance with data protection law (Article 6(1)(f)), and where applicable our legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train machine learning models. We do not carry out any automated decision making or profiling.

## Who processes your data on our behalf

We use a small number of service providers (processors) to run the Service. Each is bound by a data processing agreement.

**Supabase** hosts our database and provides authentication. Your account and planning data are stored in a Supabase project hosted in Paris, France (in the European Union).

**Vercel** hosts the web application and serves it to your browser.

**Brevo** sends transactional emails on our behalf, such as signup confirmation and password reset emails.

**Apple** distributes the iOS app through the App Store. Apple's own privacy terms apply to your App Store account; Apple does not receive your Listical planning data.

Where a provider processes data outside the UK or EEA, transfers are protected by the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

## How long we keep your data

We keep your account and planning data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your account data and all planning data from our live database. Residual copies in encrypted database backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle within 7 days. We retain the minimal deletion audit record, which does not contain your planning data, for 12 months to demonstrate compliance.

## Your rights

Under UK and EU GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a copy in a portable machine readable format, restrict or object to certain processing, and complain to a regulator.

You can delete your account, and all data with it, directly in the Service via account settings on the web app or in the iOS app. For any other request, email us at hello@tacular.app and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, or to your local supervisory authority if you are in the EU. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

## Age requirement

The Service is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly provide accounts to anyone under 18, and our systems reject signups indicating an age below 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, contact us at hello@tacular.app and we will delete it.

## Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to production data is restricted, and database rules ensure that each user can only ever read and write their own data.

## Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy we will notify you by email or by a notice in the Service before the changes take effect. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
